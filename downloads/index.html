Macry

Version 1.0.1 · requires macOS 14 or later

Download .dmg Download .zip

⚠️ macOS will say Macry "cannot be opened because Apple cannot verify it is free of malware." This is expected — Macry isn't notarized by Apple yet, but it's safe to run. Do one of these once, after installing:

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) Macry.app in Applications and choose Open, then confirm in the dialog that appears, or
  2. Run xattr -cr /Applications/Macry.app in Terminal.

Installing

Open the .dmg and drag Macry into Applications.
