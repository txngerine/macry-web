Download .dmg Download .zip

Installing

Open the .dmg and drag Macry into Applications.

First launch

Macry is signed with an ad-hoc signature, not notarized by Apple, so Gatekeeper will warn that it's from an unidentified developer.

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) Macry.app and choose Open, then confirm in the dialog, or
  2. Run xattr -cr /Applications/Macry.app in Terminal after installing.
