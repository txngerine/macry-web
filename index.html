App won't open?

Macry isn't notarized by Apple yet, so Gatekeeper may block it on first launch. If you see a warning that it "can't be opened," head to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to the Macry notice.

  • Open System Settings
  • Go to Privacy & Security
  • Scroll to the security notice about Macry and click Open Anyway
